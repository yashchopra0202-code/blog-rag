AlphaSense is a market research tool. It searches a library of filings, earnings-call transcripts, broker research, news, and expert-interview transcripts, and returns AI-generated summaries that help consultants better understand prospective clients and deepen relationships with existing ones.

Both packages, Market Intelligence and Enterprise Intelligence, come with custom pricing. The median annual contract value is $18,000 (

, September 2026).

Tableau AI, Salesforce's AI data analysis platform with a generative AI layer called Einstein, lets consultants analyze and visualize client data by asking questions.

Free Desktop Edition (local only). Standard is from $15 per user per month and Enterprise from $35. Tableau Agent is included from the Cloud+ tier.

Zapier connects more than 9,000 apps and runs multi-step workflows, AI agents, and an MCP endpoint that lets an assistant like Claude or ChatGPT take actions directly inside other tools. Consultants use Zapier to automate routine, manual tasks, like copying data across different docs or apps.

Free plan for 100 tasks per month. Professional from $19.99 per month, Team from $69 per month.

Notion is a knowledge management platform that uses docs, wikis, and databases. Its AI layer, Notion AI, helps you automate your daily consulting workflows. It creates and edits pages, transcribes and summarizes meeting notes, and searches connected apps like Slack, Google Drive, Jira, and GitHub.

Free and Plus plans include only a trial of Notion AI.  Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes, and Enterprise Search require the Business plan ($20 per member per month).

NotebookLM, recently rebranded as Gemini Notebook, is an AI-powered research assistant that uses only uploaded sources to provide answers, which reduces the risk of errors and hallucinations. Consultants use it to build reliable executive summaries and extract accurate data from reports.

Free Standard plan; paid starts at $4.99 per month. Google AI Pro: $19.99 per month.

Taplio is a LinkedIn-only content creation tool that finds post ideas, writes and schedules posts in a user's own voice, surfaces people worth engaging with, and converts that engagement into connection requests and messages.

Consultants and coaches use Taplio to build their LinkedIn presence and cultivate relationships with prospective clients.

7-day free trial with full access to the Pro tier. Plans start from $39 per month.

Canva is a user-friendly graphic design tool for non-designers. Canva AI includes a conversational AI assistant and its Magic Studio tools, and it lets consultants create professional presentation decks, charts, graphs, and other visuals.

Free version with up to 20 AI uses. Pro is US$144 per year; Business is US$250 per year per person.

Otter.ai joins or records a meeting, transcribes it with speaker identification, produces a summary with action items, and lets you search or ask questions about every past meeting.

Free Basic plan with 300 transcription minutes per month. Pro starts at $16.99 per user per month, and Business at $19.99 per user per month.

Timely is an AI time tracker built for consultancies and agencies. It automatically captures every minute you work across web and desktop apps in the background, so there’s no need to log them manually, and uses AI to draft and categorize timesheet entries.

Free trial; Starter: $11 per user per month ($9 billed yearly), for up to 5 users; Premium: $20 per user per month ($16 billed yearly); Unlimited: $28 per user per month ($22 billed yearly). Enterprise pricing is custom.

Qwilr lets consultants build web-based, interactive proposals with dynamic pricing tables, e-signature, and payment collection, and analyze prospect engagement: who viewed the proposal, where they spent time, and what they clicked.

14-day free trial. Starter: $35 per user per month billed annually, or $49 monthly. Growth $55, Scale $75.

You can't outsource all your consultant work to AI tools — not if you're serious about your career and reputation. But you can use AI tools to automate many manual tasks that consume your time, so you can focus on high-impact work.

Which ones you choose depends on the nature of your work and bottlenecks in your workflows. Most consultants will benefit from a generic AI tool like ChatGPT, but not all need to make visuals, analyze data, or create proposals.

If you're spending hours researching and verifying data to inform your consulting work, Perplexity will help.

AI tools for consultants are software that applies large language models or machine learning to perform tasks like gathering and citing evidence, synthesizing client documents, analyzing data, producing decks and diagrams, capturing meetings, tracking time, and assembling proposals.

Some, like ChatGPT, are general

, while others, like Perplexity, AlphaSense, and Qwilr, are built to perform a specific job.

No single AI tool covers the full scope of a consultant's job. They are all good for specific use cases. Perplexity or AlphaSense are the best AI tools for research, NotebookLM is the best AI tool for document synthesis, Canva AI is the best AI graphic design tool, Otter is the best AI meeting assistant, while Qwilr is the best AI proposal software.

Some free AI tools are safe to use with client data. NotebookLM, Notion, Qwilr, and Zapier don’t train their systems on uploaded content. Others, like Perplexity, train their models on your data on the free plan unless you opt out. Otter's free tier doesn't offer the opt-out option but uses only de-identified recordings.

To be on the safe side, check the plan's data statement and the client's contract before pasting anything identifiable, and anonymize the data where possible.

A consultant needs 4-10 AI tools, but the exact number depends on the work they do, workflow bottlenecks, and the resources at their disposal. For example, if they're working with a graphic designer, they won't need Canva AI, and a consultant working for a large firm isn't likely to create proposals themselves.

Large consulting firms often run internal knowledge-search tools alongside enterprise-licensed Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, or Microsoft Copilot, with consumer tool versions frequently blocked outright, and approved versions not running the frontier models.
