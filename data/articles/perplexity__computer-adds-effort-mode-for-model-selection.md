Perplexity Computer’s effort controls match model selection and reasoning to each task, with settings from Light to Ultra.

Contents

Today, we’re rolling out effort controls in

, giving people more control over how much effort it puts into their work. Using a slider, they can choose one of four settings, from Light to Ultra, and Perplexity selects the model and reasoning level for each setting.

People told us they wanted to decide how much effort a task deserved and what they were willing to spend, while leaving model selection to Perplexity. Users who prefer to choose a specific model and reasoning level can still do so in custom controls.

Users select the setting they want to use from the slider in the omnibar:

Lower settings use less expensive models. Credit usage depends on the work Computer performs.

An effort setting selects the model responsible for coordinating an assignment and how deeply it reasons. The orchestrator decides how to approach the work and delegates parts of it to supporting agents, which can use models from different providers.

One example: A memo informing an important decision may warrant deeper analysis before someone acts on its recommendation. Users can choose a higher setting for that assignment without having to choose a specific model or configuring its reasoning level themselves.

Perplexity’s experience answering billions of queries informs how we match models to work. As people use Perplexity for a wider range of assignments, we learn more about where different models perform well and where additional reasoning improves results.

We test models from different providers at different reasoning levels, comparing the quality of their work with its cost. When a lower-cost combination produces comparable results, users can keep more of their budget for assignments that benefit from deeper reasoning.

The effort controls area available on web and will be available on Android and iOS soon. Users can access the effort slider in Computer, with Light, Standard, High, and Ultra settings.

Custom controls are still available for users who want to choose a specific model and reasoning level.
