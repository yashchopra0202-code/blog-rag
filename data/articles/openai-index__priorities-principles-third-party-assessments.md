A specific assertion about a model or system’s capabilities, behavior, or safeguards that bears on its safety and can be assessed against evidence. A claim should identify the risks and conditions it addresses, along with relevant assumptions and limitations.

A structured argument, supported by evidence, explaining why a model or system’s risks are adequately managed for a specified activity, such as training, evaluation, or deployment. A safety case connects individual safety claims to the evidence supporting them and makes explicit the assumptions, uncertainties, and remaining risks that could affect its conclusions.
