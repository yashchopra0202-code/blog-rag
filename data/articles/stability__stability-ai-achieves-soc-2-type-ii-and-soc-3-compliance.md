This is an important milestone, putting Stability AI among a small number of AI leaders that have reached this high industry standard.

SOC 2 Type II is a globally recognized benchmark for security compliance in the technology sector, especially in the emerging generative AI landscape. The SOC 3 certification provides a public summary of these security controls.

The certification process involves a comprehensive examination of our security practices across five trust service criteria: security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy. An independent third-party auditor rigorously assessed our internal controls, policies, and procedures to confirm that Stability AI maintains robust data protection measures over time.

As AI becomes integrated into professional creative pipelines, organizations need assurance that their AI partners maintain enterprise-grade security standards.

For our customers, these certifications ensure peace of mind when deploying our AI tools in production environments. This is particularly important for organizations handling proprietary creative assets, brand materials, and content libraries, where protecting intellectual property and maintaining confidentiality are paramount.

We’re committed to maintaining this certification and continually improving our security posture as we deliver enterprise-grade AI offerings.
