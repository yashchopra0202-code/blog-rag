Airbnb engineers use an internal AI assistant for writing software and creating remote AI agents powered by Codex and models like GPT‑5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna. Astra helps Airbnb engineers accelerate their work by tracking down hard bugs, shaping system designs, and brainstorming engineering approaches.
