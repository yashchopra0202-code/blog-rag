Today we’re introducing The X Subscription, which brings together Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor, and X Premium in one plan. Over the next few days, we’ll make it available to everyone globally.

For the first time, one AI subscription supports the full path from thinking and creating to building and sharing.

Paid plans on The X Subscription include a generous pool of Grok model usage shared across Grok and Cursor.

Your shared pool covers Grok models whether you use them in the Grok apps, the Grok tab on X, or Cursor. With the new plans, third-party models are still available in Cursor on Premium and above. Usage is billed separately at API list prices, through top-ups.

The X Subscription is available to new customers starting today.
