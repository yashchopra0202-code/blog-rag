Running a business often means managing a wide range of responsibilities. A business owner might spend one day planning a marketing campaign and the next forecasting cash flow to handle a surge in orders. As a business grows, these tasks can become more complex and time-consuming. AI can help business owners tackle this work more efficiently so they can stay focused on moving their business forward.

specifically curated for U.S. American Express Business Card Members with a Perplexity Enterprise subscription. The collection gives business owners pre-built AI workflows that focus on everyday work needs, including tax prep, cash flow forecasting, and marketing campaign generation.

Skills are reusable sets of instructions that guide Perplexity Computer through specific tasks, including the information it needs and the output it should produce. With this collection, business owners select a task and provide details through a form, such as the focus of a campaign or the period a forecast should cover. They don’t have to write a detailed query from scratch.

The curated collection of business Skills spans finance, marketing, operations, and hiring. Business owners can begin with one task, review the result, and build from there.

Running Skills requires Computer Credits, and usage varies with the complexity of the task.

Eligible Card Members with a Perplexity Enterprise subscription (subject to auto-renewal) can open Personal CFO from the connectors page and link their American Express Business Card through Plaid to access the Skills collection. After a business owner selects a Skill and supplies the task details, Computer checks for the data it needs and asks for any missing connections to tools it needs to access.

For example, cash flow forecasting uses bank balances and transaction history to build a week-by-week forecast and identify potential cash shortfalls. A full forecast requires the business' operating bank account through Plaid. Plus, connecting an accounting tool can add invoice and bill details to improve the timing estimates.

The collection covers the following tasks, using connected business data and, where supported, uploaded files. Business owners review the results before making decisions or putting drafts to use.
