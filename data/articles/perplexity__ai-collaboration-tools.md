Notion is a highly customizable knowledge management platform that uses relational databases to store and connect your pages, documents, and dashboards. You can organize and view them as calendar entries, grids, checklists, or cards in Kanban boards.

It connects to Slack, Google Drive, and other external apps, and feeds that context into Notion's search and agents.

Identifying the problem that's costing your team the most time and energy, and finding an AI tool that solves it is a good starting point when designing your tech stack.

If communication is an issue, Slack and Loom will help. If project tracking and reporting are the challenge, have a look at Asana, Trello, ClickUp, or Monday.com. If your teams work in silos, use Notion, Confluence, or Airtable to build shared knowledge bases that act as a single source of truth.

However, AI benefits go beyond solving the known problems. As AI platforms get more potent and perform tasks we never thought possible, true competitive advantage will come from constantly exploring and adopting new capabilities that amplify your teams' productivity and efficiency.

AI collaboration tools are software platforms that use AI features to simplify and automate tasks that otherwise would be performed by people, for example, data entry, note-taking, or summarization. By relieving teams of manual tasks and allowing them to share information, they help remote teams collaborate effectively.

ClickUp lets you pick between GPT, Claude Opus, and Gemini. Airtable, Asana, Notion, and monday.com all use OpenAI and Anthropic models. Asana and monday.com add Google Gemini, and Airtable adds Meta Llama. Atlassian (Confluence and Trello) lists OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and open models like Llama and Gemma. Miro names the exact model behind each feature.

Slack says only that it uses third-party LLMs hosted in its own cloud.

Major AI collaboration vendors don't train their models on team data, but how they implement safeguards varies. Slack hosts its AI inside its own cloud so providers never see customer data, and Airtable offers Amazon Bedrock for the same reason. Loom sends only transcript text to OpenAI, never full video or audio.
