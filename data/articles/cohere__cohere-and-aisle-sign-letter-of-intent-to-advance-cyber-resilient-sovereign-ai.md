Today Cohere announced a proposed agreement to acquire AISLE, the company building autonomous systems to discover and remediate previously unknown vulnerabilities in critical software.

The proposed transaction represents another important step in Cohere's mission to build the world's leading sovereign AI platform, combining frontier AI models, agentic applications, secure deployment infrastructure, and advanced security capabilities that enable organizations to deploy AI with confidence.

It also reflects our continued investment in Europe. Following our acquisitions of Reliant AI and Aleph Alpha, the addition of AISLE would deepen our presence across the region and strengthen European AI and cybersecurity capabilities that help protect critical infrastructure, governments, and highly regulated industries around the world.

As organizations move AI from experimentation to production, security has become one of the most important factors determining successful adoption.

Enterprises and governments are increasingly navigating complex requirements around data protection, governance, regulatory compliance, software vulnerabilities, and the risks associated with more autonomous AI systems. At the same time, they are looking for AI partners that can deliver the flexibility to operate in private cloud, on-premises, and fully air-gapped environments without compromising security or control.

Addressing these security challenges requires more than powerful models. It requires a full-stack approach that brings together AI, cybersecurity, governance, and secure deployment. That's why we're excited about the opportunity to bring AISLE's expertise into Cohere.

Founded in 2024, AISLE has developed innovative AI systems capable of discovering, validating and helping remediate previously unknown vulnerabilities in complex production software. Its approach combines increasingly capable foundation models with specialized security expertise, large-scale code analysis, rigorous validation and automated remediation.

AISLE's results also highlight an important insight: the capabilities of a foundation model and the specialized system built around it can reinforce each other. By bringing AISLE's autonomous security research closer to Cohere's frontier-model development and training, we see an opportunity to build more capable cybersecurity systems while exploring how difficult, verifiable security problems can help evaluate and improve future models.

AISLE’s team is joining Cohere, with its three founders taking leadership roles spanning cybersecurity, corporate security, AI security research and frontier model development.

Ondrej Vlcek will become Senior Vice President, Cohere Cyber, and will lead the newly-established global cybersecurity business. Jaya Baloo will become Cohere’s Chief Information Security Officer, leading security across the company. Stanislav Fort will become Vice President, AI Security, joining Cohere’s core model research and training efforts and leading its AI security research.

Cohere also plans to make a substantial long-term investment in Prague, expanding AISLE’s existing presence and establishing the city as its global center for AI cybersecurity and autonomous security systems.

Europe is becoming increasingly important in the development of sovereign AI. Governments and enterprises across the region are seeking technology partners that can deliver advanced AI capabilities while meeting stringent requirements around security, privacy, compliance, and data sovereignty. Cohere has made significant investments to support those needs, and this deal would further expand our ability to serve customers across Europe and globally.

AISLE's technology will strengthen Cohere's ability to help customers build and operate secure, cyber-resilient AI systems.

By combining AISLE's autonomous vulnerability research with Cohere's frontier models and sovereign AI platform, we aim to make discovering, validating and fixing software vulnerabilities a continuous, scalable capability that helps protect critical systems.

We're excited about what we can build together and look forward to officially welcoming the AISLE team to Cohere.
